# Changelog

## 0.1.0 preview

The first private preview contains the complete educational investigation.

- Replay Tohoku 2011, Maule 2010, and Okhotsk 2013 against 21 real station recordings.
- Explore a textured 3D Earth, a fixed quarter cutaway, exact ray paths, and sampled P, S, and PKIKP fronts.
- Compare predicted station arrivals with archived manual P picks on one clock.
- Fit a bounded Tohoku source at fixed depth and inspect its disagreement with the historical catalog.
- Inspect record sections and sampled travel-time diagrams.
- Reproduce an experiment with an identity-bound URL and export its current results.
- Build local Rust and WebAssembly calculations with frozen input provenance and reproducible checks.

The [scientific limits](docs/numerical-contract.md), [location method](docs/location-experiment.md), and [data notices](THIRD_PARTY.md) define the scope. This preview does not establish complete TauP parity, a full seismic wavefield, or operational accuracy.
