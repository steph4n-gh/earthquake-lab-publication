# Community conduct

Discuss the code, data, and scientific claims with care and respect. Explain disagreements with evidence. Help contributors reproduce problems.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, disclosure of private information, and spam are unacceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect contributors.

To report a conduct concern, ask the repository maintainer for a private channel. Do not post another person's private information in an issue.
